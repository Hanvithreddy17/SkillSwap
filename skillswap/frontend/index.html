How SkillSwap Works

Simple 3-step peer exchange designed for college students

💡
Step 1

List Your Skills

Add the skills you are good at (Offer) and the skills you are eager to pick up (Wanted) along with your proficiency level.

🔄
Step 2

Discover Reciprocal Matches

Our smart matching query finds peers where Student A offers what Student B wants, and Student B offers what Student A wants!

🚀
Step 3

Swap, Learn & Review

Accept requests, schedule online or campus study sessions, and review each other to build your academic credibility.
